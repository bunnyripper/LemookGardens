Activities

A Day at Lemook

Come for the stay. Stay for everything in between.

01 Play

Let the day begin outdoors.

From energetic games to easygoing outdoor fun, there is plenty of space to play, explore, and enjoy the garden.

Morning energy

Kids' Playground

A dedicated space for little ones to climb, swing, slide, and enjoy the outdoors while families relax nearby

01

Trampoline

Bounce, laugh, and burn off some energy with a fun outdoor activity for kids and the young at heart

02

Lawn Games

Make the most of the open lawns with simple games, friendly competition, and plenty of room for groups to get involved.

03

Badminton

Pick up a racket and enjoy a relaxed game outdoors, whether you're playing for fun or keeping score.

04

Mini Golf

Test your skills on our well-maintained mini-golf course, where you can enjoy a leisurely round with family and friends.

02 Unwind

Slow down. Stay awhile.

Take a break from the pace of the day with games made for easy conversation, friendly competition, and spending time together.

01

Indoor Games

Challenge your friends to a game of pool, darts, or cards and enjoy some friendly competition indoors.

02

Outdoor Board Games

Settle in for a relaxed game of chess or other board games while enjoying the fresh air and open garden around you.

03 Gather

When the sun goes down, come together.

As the day winds down, gather around good food, warm fires, and the simple pleasure of spending time together.

The evening

Bonfire Pit

Gather around the fire as the evening sets in, share stories, enjoy the warmth, and make the most of the night together.

01

Barbecue Zone

Fire up the grill and enjoy a laid-back barbecue with family and friends, with plenty of space to cook, eat, and unwind.

02

Picnic Tables

Relax and enjoy a meal outdoors on our comfortable picnic tables, perfect for family gatherings or casual get-togethers.

Experience Lemook

Whether you're here for a few hours or staying the night, there is always something to enjoy. Come make your own day at Lemook.
