Our Story

A place with a story worth discovering.

Lemook Gardens was created as a place to step away from the pace of everyday life and enjoy a little more time outdoors. What began as a simple garden destination has grown into a place where people come to stay, dine, celebrate and spend time together.

Today, Lemook remains a peaceful escape from the city, with spaces designed for quiet moments, shared experiences and special occasions.
